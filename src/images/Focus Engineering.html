Expertise

At Focus Engineering, we strive to be recognized as a leading engineering company, known for our innovative work methods, advanced technology, and highly skilled team. With extensive expertise in design and decades of industry experience, we develop products tailored to our clients' specific requirements while adhering to relevant specifications and international standards.

DESIGNING

Our design process is meticulous—once the client approves our design, we create a prototype that undergoes rigorous testing and quality checks before moving to mass production.

We utilize industry-leading software, including AutoCAD for 2D drawings and PowerShape & PPI for 3D modeling, to design high-precision connectors, hardware components, and industrial parts.

At Focus Engineering, precision, quality, and customer satisfaction are at the core of everything we do.

Advanced casting capabilities

Our casting facility is fully equipped with state-of-the-art machinery, including furnaces of various capacities, sand mixers, die fixtures, an extensive range of molds, air compressors, and all necessary tools and equipment.

Led by a team of highly skilled professionals, we specialize in both sand casting and die casting, adhering to international standards to ensure precision, durability, and superior quality in every product.

We offer expertise in casting the following metals and alloys:

  • Aluminum Alloy Casting
  • Bronze Casting
  • Copper Alloy Casting
  • Ferrous Metals Casting

At Focus Engineering, we combine advanced technology with industry expertise to deliver high-quality castings that meet the exacting demands of our clients.

Precision forging

We manufacture high-quality steel components for string assemblies used in Extra High Voltage (EHV) Overhead Transmission Lines. Utilizing advanced forging techniques, we ensure that each part is produced to exact specifications and meets international standards for strength, durability, and performance.

At Focus Engineering, our commitment to precision engineering guarantees reliable and long-lasting solutions for the power transmission industry.

Precision MAchining

Our machining section is fully equipped with advanced machinery, including lathe machines, milling machines, drill machines, cutters, sanding and grinding machines, along with all necessary tools and equipment.

After the casting process, each product undergoes precision machining to achieve its final shape, ensuring accuracy, functionality, and compliance with the required specifications. At Focus Engineering, we prioritize quality and precision to deliver superior components tailored to industry needs.

Welding & Fabrication

Our welding and fabrication workshop is fully equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including large and small welding tables, AC & DC welding plants, cutters, presses, grinding machines, drilling machines, and all essential tools and equipment.

We specialize in manufacturing earthing platforms, steel support structures, and aluminum-welded transmission line hardware components for string assemblies. With a focus on precision and durability, our expert team ensures that every product meets industry standards and client specifications.

galvanizing

All ferrous metal components used in string assemblies for Grid Stations and Transmission Line Hardware undergo a rigorous galvanization process, ensuring compliance with required specifications and international standards.

Additionally, earthing platforms and steel support structures are galvanized in accordance with NTDC/WAPDA specifications, enhancing their durability and corrosion resistance for long-term reliability in the field.

comprehensive testing

To ensure the highest quality and reliability, we conduct Temperature Rise Tests for connectors using a 2000 Amp Current Transformer. Our testing facility is equipped with advanced instruments to guarantee precise measurements and compliance with industry standards.

Our testing equipment includes:

  • Ammeter – For accurate current measurement in the testing loop.
  • Brinell Hardness Tester – To assess material hardness and durability.
  • Electronic Thermometers – For precise temperature measurement of connectors and associated conductors.

At Focus Engineering, our rigorous testing processes ensure that every product meets the highest performance and safety standards.
